Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

DIU mail agent ("the app") is a personal tool. This policy explains what Google user data it accesses and how that data is used.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Read and modify Gmail messages (gmail.modify): to read incoming messages and apply labels or mark them as read.
  • Send email (gmail.send): to send replies from your account.

How the data is used

Message content is used only to provide the app's features: reading your mail, drafting replies, and sending them. To draft replies, the text of a message may be sent to a third-party AI provider (Groq) for processing. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any model by the app's developer.

Storage

The app runs on the developer's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored in a local file on that computer. The app does not send Gmail data to any server owned by the developer, and it does not maintain a database of your email.

Sharing

Your Google user data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone except the AI provider named above, solely to generate replies. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the local token file to remove stored credentials.
